How to Play DETECTIVE.PK
You are a detective investigating a murder. Gather evidence, interrogate suspects, and make your arrest before time runs out. Every clue matters — read carefully.
STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE
1
🔴 CRIME SCENE — Find all 6 clues
Click the red pulsing circles on the crime scene. Each one reveals a piece of evidence. Read the description AND the analysis — the analysis tells you what the clue means.
2
📁 EVIDENCE LOCKER — Review what you found
All collected evidence appears here. Click any found card to re-read it. Evidence you haven't found yet is locked — go back to the crime scene.
3
👤 SUSPECTS — Interrogate everyone
Click INTERROGATE on each suspect. Ask questions by clicking them. Some questions are locked — collect the required evidence first. Watch the Psychological Pressure meter rise as suspects get nervous. Use "PRESENT EVIDENCE" to confront them directly with clues.
4
🔗 DEDUCTION BOARD — Connect the dots
Drag evidence and suspect cards around. Click CONNECT, then click two cards to draw a red string between them — linking a clue to the person it implicates.
5
🚔 MAKE ARREST — When you're sure
Once you have at least 3 clues, the MAKE ARREST button activates. Pick your suspect, select 3 key pieces of evidence that prove their guilt, and confirm. You get one chance — choose wisely.
TIPS FOR SOLVING THE CASE
Read the analysis — each clue has a plain-English "what this means" section. Don't skip it.
High pressure = contradictions — when a suspect's meter is high, their answers start breaking down. Ask harder questions.
Present evidence during interrogation — the left panel lets you confront suspects with specific clues to unlock reactions.
Use HINTS sparingly — you only get 3. Each hint nudges you toward the key evidence path.
Alibis can be broken — every innocent suspect has a solid alibi. The guilty one has a flaw. Find it in the evidence.
